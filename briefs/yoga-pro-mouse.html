How rivals win

  1. Budget silent eats volume with price + the word “silent.”
  2. Mid fashion / dual-mode (Pebble-class) eats design and brand habit.
  3. Vertical premium eats a pain point (wrists) at a higher AOV.

Recommendations

  1. Pricing: Trial promo or list into $24.99–$27.99; or keep ~$32 but use bundle / timed coupon so landed price lands in that band.
  2. Title / images: Within the first ~80 characters, make Pro ≠ Silent obvious (charging, metal scroll wheel, programmable, dual-mode).
  3. Reviews: Push substantive reviews (aim 100+ useful first)—stars alone aren’t enough.
  4. Ads / keywords: Tighten to Yoga / Pro / charging mouse; cut case/sleeve noise waste.
  5. Line split: Silent owns entry price; Pro owns advanced—cross-link listings, don’t collide on claims.
