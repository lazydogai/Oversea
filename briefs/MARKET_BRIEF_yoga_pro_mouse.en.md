## One-page takeaway (read this first)

Under this keyword, the real fight is “Bluetooth / silent / mid-price mice,” not a Yoga Pro–only pool.  
Price band is wide: **$8.99–$96.50**, median only **$24.99**—your seed at **$31.99** already sits above most rivals (**36 of 47** priced listings are cheaper).

Rank #1 ≠ best seller: the seed does ~2K+/mo, while budget silent can hit 9K+ and premium vertical ergonomics 10K+.  
Same-brand Yoga Silent siblings (~$22–$28) crowd the SERP and siphon Pro clicks.

**If you sell / operate this ASIN:** the urgent call is **positioning + price**—either move closer to the **$25–$28** hub, or make the title / A+ crystal clear on why Pro costs more (dual-mode, programmable, metal scroll wheel, charging) and keep Silent distinct. Otherwise shoppers buy the cheaper neighbor.

## Three price tiers (how the pie splits)

**Budget $9–$20**  
Basic wireless / silent. Example: WL310 ~**$13.99**, ~**9K+**/mo—proves “cheap + silent” has real volume.  
→ Don’t price-war this tier unless you launch a sub-SKU.

**Mid $20–$40 (main battlefield)**  
Bluetooth, DPI, silent, dual-mode. Seed Yoga Pro **$31.99** sits toward the upper edge; same-line Silent often **$22–$28**; Logitech Pebble 2 ~**$24**, 4K+.  
→ In comparison shopping, Pro must win on the **spec story**, not on “rank #1.”

**Premium $40+**  
Vertical ergonomics, gaming, brand ladders. Lift Vertical ~**$66**, **10K+**—a different need pool (wrist health), only partly overlapping Yoga Pro.  
→ Borrow messaging; you don’t have to match price.

## Where the seed sits

- **Exposure:** #1 under the keyword—good news.  
- **Demand feel:** ~2K+/mo, behind budget smash hits and vertical smash hits—traffic yes, harvest incomplete.  
- **Stars:** 4.4 is fine, but public SERP review-count signals look weaker than some mature SKUs; trust needs work.  
- **Family risk:** Silent priced nearer the median with similar titles—easy cannibalization.

Plain English: **people see you; they don’t always pick you.**

## How rivals win (how a human would say it)

1. **Budget silent** eats volume with price + the word “silent.”  
2. **Mid fashion / dual-mode** (Pebble-class) eats design and brand habit.  
3. **Vertical premium** eats a pain point (wrists) at a higher AOV.  
4. **Own Silent line** stacks on the same SERP—internal competition; need product-line roles.

---

## Recommendations (actionable, short)

1. **Pricing:** Trial promo or list into **$24.99–$27.99**; or keep ~$32 but use bundle / timed coupon so **landed price** lands in that band.  
2. **Title / images:** Within the first ~80 characters, make Pro ≠ Silent obvious (charging, metal scroll wheel, programmable, dual-mode).  
3. **Reviews:** Push substantive reviews (aim 100+ useful first)—stars alone aren’t enough.  
4. **Ads / keywords:** Tighten to Yoga / Pro / charging mouse; cut case/sleeve noise waste.  
5. **Line split:** Silent owns entry price; Pro owns advanced—cross-link listings, don’t collide on claims.
